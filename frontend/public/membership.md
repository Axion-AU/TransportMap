# Membership — Fusion Party Australia

This machine-readable file describes the membership structure and pricing for Fusion Party Australia. Membership funds public transport modeling, data analysis, and transport inequality advocacy (including the Transport Score project).

## Membership Pricing Tiers

### Free
- **Price:** $0.00 AUD / year
- **Includes:** Access to suburb lookups, full Melbourne transit rankings, and the interactive stop-level map.

### Concession
- **Price:** $17.27 AUD / year (indexed to Victorian statutory fee units)
- **Includes:** Full membership rights, voting power in policy development, and direct funding for transport inequality campaigns.
- **Audience:** Students, pensioners, healthcare card holders.
- **Conversion Href:** /join

### Standard
- **Price:** $69.08 AUD / year (indexed to Victorian statutory fee units)
- **Includes:** Full membership rights, voting power in policy development, and direct funding for transport inequality campaigns.
- **Audience:** Standard Victorian residents.
- **Conversion Href:** /join
